## Question

Which keyboard-device browsers must be supported, and what observable play quality is acceptable under latency, jitter, packet loss, tab suspension, and disconnects? Set measurable round fairness, responsiveness, correction, and recovery targets, plus expected concurrent rooms and testing conditions. Distinguish a supported environment from degraded behavior. No ranking or sophisticated anti-cheat.
