## Question

What minimal deployment and runtime arrangement supports in-memory rooms and the chosen transport? Define server process ownership, room cleanup, graceful shutdown/restart behavior, certificate needs, development setup, and observability sufficient to judge play quality. Decide whether a single server instance is the intended first-version limit and how capacity exhaustion appears to players. Databases and deployment execution are outside this map.
