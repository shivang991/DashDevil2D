## Question

What precisely does 'the player who provided the second-to-last input loses HP' mean? Define qualifying intents, the shared input history, ordering/time basis, collisions before enough inputs exist, repeated/held inputs, simultaneous inputs, late arrival, overlap during a lane change, and repeated contact. Decide damage and invincibility behavior and test concrete two-player timelines. Keep ordering authoritative; do not assume arrival order equals player action order.
