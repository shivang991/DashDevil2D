## Question

Which observable Long Drive behaviors must remain equivalent through restructuring, and what counts as acceptable equivalence? Inventory keyboard behavior, pause/resume, countdown, restart, scoring, difficulty progression, damage and invincibility, audio, animation, and game-over presentation against the current client. Touch controls are explicitly removed. Distinguish gameplay requirements from incidental canvas-dependent behavior; define how equivalence will be checked.
