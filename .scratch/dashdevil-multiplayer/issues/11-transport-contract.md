## Question

Based on feasibility findings and supported play environments, choose WebTransport reliable streams, WebSockets, or a documented fallback combination. Specify responsibilities for HTTP(S), WebSockets and gameplay channels, stream separation, connection establishment and teardown, ordering/backpressure expectations, and failure behavior. Record genuine trade-offs rather than retaining the proposed transport by default.
